# Desktop projects

## Family Garden

I built a Rust editor for composing botanical illustrations from reusable flower assets and original vector artwork. The editor keeps position-specific assignments editable. A flower can be moved or rotated independently while its stem and leaves stay in place. Saved projects can be reopened and exported in batches.

A verified export batch contained **240 SVG files** covering 24 bouquets. The output preserved the original stem paths and made each position available as a separate vector file.

[Explore the related web bouquet generator](https://github.com/lGiaroli/birth-month-bouquet-generator)

## Bruma

I built a Windows PDF reader around a common reading task: following references and returning to the original passage without losing your place. Bruma navigates using the PDF's internal destinations and retains the source page and scroll position.

I verified cross-page and same-page references, multiline links, and exact scroll restoration after returning from a reference.
